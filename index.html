ABOUT US

Why Chicken 88?

We're Roxas, Isabela's favorite spot for boneless fried chicken —
serving quality flavors since day one.

  • 100% Boneless

    Every peice is carefully deboned so you can enjoy crispy, juicy fired chicken without the hassle. Pure meat, pure flavor.

  • 12 Signature Flavors

    From Honey Butter to Fiery Buffalo, we offer a flavor for every craving. Mix and match in out boxes and party trays.

  • "Sulit Meals

    Unlimited rice & soup with every Sulit Meal. No sharing, just pure satisfaction. Perfect for the hungry Pinoy.

  • Party-Ready Trays

    Up to 5 flavors in one tray. Whether it's 30, 40 or 50 pieces, we make every gathering extra special.

  • Community Favorite

    With 6.1K likes and followers, we're proud to be Roxas, Isabela's go-to spot for boneless fried chicken.

    We also serve silog, pasta, burgers, coffee, and bubble tea ‐ a complete dining experience for everyone.
